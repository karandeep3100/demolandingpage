III

The House
on Hansi Road

Rakesh Jewellers has been a fixture of Braham Nagar, Karnal — a shop where families return for the second and third generation's weddings. We keep the old ways: open weighing, honest making charges, and a jeweller who remembers your name.

What changes is the design. Our karigars work in both the classic temple-and-jadau idiom and in clean modern silhouettes — so a piece bought today still feels right in twenty years.
